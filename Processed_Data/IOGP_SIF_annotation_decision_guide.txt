IOGP SIF ANNOTATION — LABELING DECISION GUIDE

1. Read the Narrative, What_Went_Wrong, Causal_Factors and Corrective_Actions together.
2. Identify the actual hazard described by the source.
3. Identify the barrier/control that should have prevented the event.
4. Identify the barrier failure only when the source supports it.
5. Determine the credible potential consequence, not just the actual injury.
6. Assign:
   - SIF Potential
   - Non-SIF Potential
   - Needs Review
7. Prefer the IOGP Life-Saving Rule already stated in the source.
8. Use Secondary_LSR only when clearly supported.
9. Write a short evidence-based SIF_Reason.
10. Use Needs Review / Insufficient Evidence rather than guessing.

IMPORTANT:
- A near miss with no injury can still be SIF Potential.
- Do not use actual injury severity alone to decide SIF status.
- Do not treat every Fatal/HPE/FPI source category as automatic SIF ground truth.
- Preserve the original report text.
- These annotations should be reviewed by a safety-domain/HSE reviewer before final model training.
